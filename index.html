Massachusetts Demoractic Primary Difference in Votes

Ed Markey swept the 2026 democratic primary in Massachusetts, winning a majority of the votes in every single county over Seth Moulton.

The map demonstrates the difference in votes, with the greener counties being a closer margin compared to the blue counties.

<;

The county with the highest difference was Middlesex county, where 79,136 more people voted for Markey over Moulton.

The county with the lowest difference was Nantucket, where only 248 more people voted for Markey over Moulton. However, this is likey due to Nantucket county having fewer people than any other county in Massachusetts.
